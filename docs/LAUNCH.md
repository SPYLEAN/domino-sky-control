# LinkedIn launch copy

One airport closes for 90 minutes. What happens to the rest of India?

We built **Domino Sky: Control** at the hackathon to answer exactly that, using only the organisers' dataset: 16 airports, 70 flights, 16 aircraft, 21 connection groups and 16 crews.

✈️ Close any airport for any window, and a deterministic engine works out how the delay travels with each aircraft from city to city: ground holds, 30-minute turnarounds, missed connections and crew duty limits.
🔎 Click any flight to see why it was hit. The Reasoning Lens goes from a plain-English answer, to the causal chain, to the exact formula.
🧭 A recovery optimizer simulates every feasible dispatcher action (holding flights for connecting passengers, cancelling round trips, swapping in a spare aircraft at its real base with a legal crew) and ranks them against a transparent objective. When doing nothing is best, it says so.
🎬 The Domino Story replays the disruption in seven scenes, and every sentence comes from the engine's event ledger.
🛡️ AI explains but never calculates. Any AI response that contains a number or an invented flight is rejected.

Predict the ripple. Break the chain.
